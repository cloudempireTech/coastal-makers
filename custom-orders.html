How It Works

The 4-Step Production Pipeline

Streamlined, transparent, and built for speed and precision.

01

File & Brief Intake

Send us your 3D files (STL, OBJ, 3MF, STEP) or dimensional sketches with intended material requirements via WhatsApp.

02

DFM Optimization

We audit overhangs, wall thicknesses, and tolerances for Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFAM) to ensure flawless finish.

03

Precision Print Farm

Executed on calibrated enclosed CoreXY printers at 0.08mm – 0.16mm layer height with real-time optical layer monitoring.

04

QC & Sydney Delivery

Support removal, deburring, heat-set insert installation, and hand inspection before same-day Sydney courier or pickup.
