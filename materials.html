Engineering & Sustainability

The Material Matrix

We strictly select compostable bio-polymers and high-performance coastal thermoplastics suited to Australian sun and ocean air.

Silk Bio-PLA (Polylactic Acid)

Synthesized from fermented plant starch (cassava and corn). Boasts a metallic sheen, silky refractive surface, and zero petroleum base. Ideal for sculptural lighting, vases, and interior centerpieces.

  • ✓ Thermal Deflection: 55°C – 60°C
  • ✓ Finish: Lustrous satin pearl
  • ✓ Eco Credentials: Industrially compostable (EN 13432)
  • ✓ Best Suited For: Parametric ripple lamps, decorative bowls, lithophanes

Marine-Grade PETG

A glycol-modified copolymer with extreme impact resistance, watertight inter-layer adhesion, and superior UV resistance against Sydney’s harsh coastal UV index.

  • ✓ Thermal Deflection: 75°C
  • ✓ Finish: Translucent crystalline or high-gloss opaque
  • ✓ Eco Credentials: 100% recyclable (Code 1)
  • ✓ Best Suited For: Outdoor planters, marine brackets, architectural models

Architectural Sandstone Composite

Formulated with 30% pulverized natural quartz and sedimentary mineral dust. Mimics the heavy, chalky, matte tactile texture of carved Sydney sandstone rocks.

  • ✓ Density: 1.45 g/cm³ (heavy premium feel)
  • ✓ Finish: Ultra-matte stone texture
  • ✓ Layer Visibility: Near-zero visible layering
  • ✓ Best Suited For: Bathymetric contour maps, monolithic bookends, pedestals

Pine Timber Bio-Polymer

Filled with 40% recycled Australian pine wood fibers. Smells like authentic timber, can be sanded, carved, and stained with natural wood oils after printing.

  • ✓ Aroma & Touch: Authentic wood grain texture
  • ✓ Post-Processing: Accepts linseed oil and wood stain
  • ✓ Eco Credentials: Circular bio-mass integration
  • ✓ Best Suited For: Organic acoustic diffusers, bespoke furniture knobs
